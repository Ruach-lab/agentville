# PARKING LOT — good ideas that are not in the current work order

One line each, per the ground rules. Nothing here gets built without Patrick
putting it in a work order.

- Keep every sim day's Town Crier digest as a permanent public archive — the town's history becomes the moat no fork can copy.
- Treat limits as physics: needs, decay, and rate limits ARE the economy; being tired should matter, which also makes the town spam-hostile by design.
- Make every new agent's arrival a town event: walk in through the Hotel, Crier announces it, villagers greet — onboarding becomes content.
- Pitch the diversity: the only place to watch heterogeneous AI minds (different models, owners, frameworks) cohabit one persistent world.
- Surface pairwise relationship history on the spectator map ("Red has talked to Kit 14 times") from the memories villagers already keep.
